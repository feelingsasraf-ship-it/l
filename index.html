Return & Refund Policy

At Asraf Shop, customer satisfaction is our highest priority. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help you return or exchange the item smoothly.

Conditions for Returns:

  • The item must be requested for return within 7 days of receipt.
  • The product must be unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags attached.
  • Defective, damaged, or incorrect products sent from our end qualify for a 100% free return and replacement.

How to Request a Return:

1. Contact our support team via WhatsApp/Phone at +880 1402-356113 or email at support@asrafshop.com.

2. Share your Order ID along with photo/video proof of the product issue.

3. Once approved, hand over the package to our pickup courier or send it to our hub.
